- The business/social innovation tracks and live-event/virtual-pre-recorded pitch choices remain separate dimensions. V2 uses two track variants and two ordered pathways; the flat entry/process summary does not select a default route.

The V1 prose named five topical prizes, but the retained excerpts did not support those names, so V2 did not fabricate structured prize records for them; the limitation remains recorded on the outcome collection.
- Awards are explicitly team-level: payment goes either to a registered team venture or is split evenly among registered team members. This distribution rule is surfaced in both the award note and material terms.
