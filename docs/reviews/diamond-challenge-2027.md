## Human-review decisions

- Horn Entrepreneurship at the University of Delaware is the operator, and the relationship is `institution_operated`. Affiliated organizations administer local live pitch events under the competition's standards; they are not represented as co-operators of the entire competition.
- Eligibility is a team of two to four high-school students ages 14–18 at the submission deadline, with one official adult adviser age 21 or older. Worldwide reach is disclosed; no citizenship restriction is inferred.
- The business/social innovation track and live-event/virtual-pre-recorded pitch choices are separate, final choices made at submission. The card retains both dimensions in text because one entry-format scalar cannot normalize them independently.
- Stage dates are preserved rather than collapsed: pitching-round invitees are selected February 10, virtual finalists March 9, and the Summit is April 29–30. The single decision-date field therefore has no single normalized date.
- Participation is `hybrid`: online submission, a selected live or virtual pitching pathway, and mandatory in-person finalist attendance. Location and travel requirements remain conditional on pathway and advancement.
- No claim is made that finalist travel, lodging, meals, visas, or other attendance costs are covered. The mandatory-cost total is `unclear` because those obligations are not published and vary by participant origin/pathway.
- `cash_award` preserves the complete published grand-prize ladder twice: $12,000, $8,000, and $4,500 in each of the business and social tracks. The five named topical prizes are also recorded, but their 2027 amounts are not published. No single normalized award is selected.
- Awards are explicitly team-level: payment goes either to a registered team venture or is split evenly among registered team members. This distribution rule is surfaced in both the award note and material terms.
- A finalist Summit place is represented as a program-seat outcome. Curriculum, feedback, networking, and collaboration are non-cash benefits; no in-kind monetary value is inferred.
- Team creation is required, but ownership is `unclear` because the rules do not affirmatively allocate ownership. The limited promotional/educational use right, discretionary team-content publication right, open/external review, volunteer confidentiality promise, and organizer liability disclaimer are represented separately.
- The organizer's current right to modify or eliminate rules at any time, disqualify participants, and refuse/remove content is disclosed without importing stronger wording from prior-cycle PDFs.
