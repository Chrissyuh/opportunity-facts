All seven pages appear once in `sourcePagesChecked`; evidence metadata reuses those records exactly.

## Human-review decisions

- QuestBridge administers the application/match; 55 partner colleges make admission decisions and directly provide Match Scholarships.
- The partner set is represented as a multi-institution relationship, not one named university or QuestBridge-operated college.
- Ranked Match and QuestBridge Regular Decision are separate pathways with shared application/finalist stages.
- The Match Scholarship is restricted partner-funded cost-of-attendance support, not cash paid to the student.
- College ranking is generally binding if matched, with MIT's stated exception preserved in material terms.
- The application itself costs $0. Later college cost for students who do not receive a Match Scholarship is outside that application-cost total.
- All 59 summary fields and every V2 structured collection were assessed; deterministic projection and evidence-reference checks pass.

Flat-fact statuses: 28 `disclosed`, 31 `not_found`.

## Unresolved factual ambiguity

- Individual college-partner aid packages and requirements vary and were not expanded into 55 institution records.
- A single scholarship dollar amount is not universal; the official page describes full cost and a value over $360,000 rather than one cash payment.
- Regular Decision aid may differ from the guaranteed Match Scholarship.
